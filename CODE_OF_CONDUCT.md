# Code of Conduct

Floret is for artists and creative coders of every background and level.
Everyone should feel welcome to ask a question, share a patch or report a
bug without being talked down to.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intentions.
- Be specific and constructive when you give feedback, including when
  something is not working for you.
- Respect people's time, skill level and ideas.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, and no
  sharing of other people's private information.
- Keep to the topic in issues and pull requests.

## Where this applies

In the issue tracker, pull requests, email to the project, and anywhere
else people speak for or about Floret.

## Enforcement

Comments, issues or pull requests that break these rules can be edited,
closed or removed, and people who keep breaking them can be blocked from
the project. To report a problem, email
[Floret.make@gmail.com](mailto:Floret.make@gmail.com). Reports are handled
privately.

## Where it comes from

This is a plain-language summary in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which applies to the project in full.
